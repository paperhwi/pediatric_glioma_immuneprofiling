# Changes reflected in the revised manuscript

The final submission uses the original locked retained-input partition as primary (122/144/77), and labels within-n343 re-standardization (116/149/78) explicitly as a preprocessing sensitivity. Existing Table1, Figures 1–7, differential-expression and cellular/spatial state comparisons remain based on the primary assignments. They must not be described as recalculated using the nine changed sensitivity labels.

Methods now distinguish the paired scaling check from signature re-scoring, preserve the 25-seed assignment procedure, and retain the separately specified k-diagnostic procedure. Results report 334/343 label agreement, ARI 0.915, the nine transitions, greater seed stability, and the continuing k2-versus-gap-k3 distinction.

Methods and Results also report categorical RNA-library control and library-stratified continuous-axis Cox models, with the cohort-wide SD fixed. The primary association attenuates from HR 0.827/P 0.0095 to HR 0.861 (95% CI 0.726–1.022)/P 0.088 after library adjustment; stratification and autopsy exclusion are retained. Abstract, Discussion and Conclusion no longer imply that the continuous axis has an established survival association independent of library preparation.

Publication numbering is Figures S1–S6 and Supplementary Tables S1–S6. FiguresS5/S6 and TableS6 are provided here. The submission's full manuscript, private clinical input and individual survival timelines are not distributed in this repository.

Gene-source descriptions were also clarified in the current Supplementary Table S3 without changing gene membership or scores. Study-curated/extended lists are distinguished from exact published profiles; the historical Wang2017 attribution remains unverified and is retained as an internal identifier. Inhibitory-receptor expression is not treated as definitive terminal exhaustion. Discussion positioning includes prior pediatric immune-classification studies and does not claim the first classification or automatic patient-independent validation.
