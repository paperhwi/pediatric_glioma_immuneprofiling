The final n = 343 analysis code, canonical immune-state assignments, re-standardized sensitivity assignments, specimen-exclusion list, aggregate survival-origin correction audit and rules, signature definitions and provenance audit, final figure files, and documentation of changes from the submitted analysis are available at https://github.com/paperhwi/pediatric_glioma_immuneprofiling/releases/tag/v2.0.2.
