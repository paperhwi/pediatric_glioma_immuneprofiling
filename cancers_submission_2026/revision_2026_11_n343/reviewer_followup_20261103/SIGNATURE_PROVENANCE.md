# Signature provenance audit

Current author-year identifiers denote literature-informed, study-curated lists unless exact equality to a published list is documented. Gene membership and all scores are unchanged. The neutral current name is `Cytotoxic_Lymphocyte_Curated`; its historical alias is documented only to trace earlier files. No original Wang2017 paper is claimed for that list.

## Keren-Shaul et al. 2017

Source: [Keren-Shaul et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2017.05.018). Locator: Figure 1E; Figure 6; Table S2. 16-gene DAM-related study-curated subset; not the complete differential-gene list; mouse context and CCL6 limitation retained.

## Klemm et al. 2020

Source: [Klemm et al. 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8558904/). Locator: Figure 2; Figure S2E. 16-gene microglia and 16-gene MDM lists are literature-informed curated markers; exact equality to the paper deconvolution sets not established.

## Pombo Antunes et al. 2021

Source: [Pombo Antunes et al. 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-020-00789-y). Locator: Figure 1; Supplementary Tables 2 and 3. 14-gene MgTAM and 15-gene MoTAM fixed GMT lists are study-curated; no exact source list matching these sizes established.

## Friebel et al. 2020

Source: [Friebel et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2020.04.055). Locator: Figure 6A. Protein-based Treg phenotyping provides biological context; it is not an exact ten-gene transcriptomic signature.

The full exact memberships, GMT descriptions, source-paper DOIs, locators and equality status are in the updated Supplementary Table S3 (`Signature_provenance_audit`). For the neutral cytotoxic list, the historical AUROC of 0.075 used its original myeloid target; it is retained as an audit estimate and must not be interpreted as AUROC against the new lymphocyte-associated name.

Audit access: original publisher Figure 6A was visually checked; the Antunes supplementary tables were downloaded from the publisher and inspected. Primary Cell full-text access was intermittently blocked; no inaccessible source was treated as proof of exact list equality. Figure/table locators indicate the biological context and do not imply that the complete current GMT was transcribed from that item.
